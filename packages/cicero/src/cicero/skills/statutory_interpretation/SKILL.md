# Statutory Interpretation Skill

## Canons of Construction
1. **Plain Meaning Rule:** When language is clear and unambiguous, judicial inquiry ends.
2. **Noscitur a Sociis:** Words are understood in context with neighboring terms.
3. **Ejusdem Generis:** General words following specific lists are construed in kind.
4. **Expressio Unius:** Expression of one thing implies exclusion of others.
