# Authority Classification Skill

## 1. Hierarchy of Authority
1. **Mandatory Primary Authority:**
   - US Supreme Court precedents on federal constitutional or statutory questions.
   - Circuit Court of Appeals decisions within the target circuit.
   - State Supreme Court decisions on substantive state law issues.
2. **Persuasive Primary Authority:**
   - Decisions from sister federal circuits.
   - Decisions from federal district courts.
   - Appellate decisions from foreign states.
3. **Secondary Authority:**
   - Restatements of Law, law review articles, and legal treatises.

## 2. Evidentiary Standards
- Never present persuasive authority as binding precedent.
- Explicitly note circuit splits when addressing questions of first impression.
